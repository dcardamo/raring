Compatibility

Will it work with my watch?

Raring reads your Garmin account, not your watch. The weekly plan works with most watches that sync to Garmin Connect. Strength features depend on what your watch records and downloads.

  • Tested

    We’ve used it on this watch.

  • Expected to work

    The watch has the Garmin features it needs. Nobody has confirmed it yet.

  • Unknown

    No reports yet. Yours would help.

What each feature needs

  • Weekly plan

    Intensity minutes, heart-rate zones and sleep in Garmin Connect.

  • Set read-back

    A Strength activity that records sets, reps and weights.

  • Set correction

    Edits sets on the activity in your Garmin account. Works wherever read-back does.

  • Workout push

    Downloads workouts from Garmin’s calendar. Up to about 100 steps each, rests included.
