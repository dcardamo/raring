Compatibility

Will it work with my watch?

Raring reads your Garmin account, not your watch. Strength sets depend on what your watch records: start a Strength activity and press lap after each set.

  • Tested

    We’ve used it on this watch.

  • Partly tested

    Some of it works on this watch. The rest hasn’t been checked yet.

  • Expected to work

    The watch has the Garmin features it needs. Nobody has confirmed it yet.

  • Unknown

    No reports yet. Yours would help.

What each feature needs

  • Set read-back

    A Strength activity that records sets, reps and weights. Raring shows them, flags sets that look miscounted and counts sets per muscle.

If your watch did not identify separate sets, the activity may contain only one timed entry with no reps or exercise. Mark sets on the watch and check the recorded details. Activities entered without a watch may have no sets at all.

Garmin decides what each watch records, and it can change its service at any time. A watch that works today could stop.
